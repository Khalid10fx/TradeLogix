TradeCycle Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

TradeCycle is a trading journal designed primarily to keep journal information available on your device. Journal entries, notes, screenshots, preferences and related local data may be stored in your browser or device storage.

CSV imports are processed in the browser. Optional third-party features, when used, may process information according to their own policies.

TradeCycle is not a broker or financial adviser. Protect your device and exported backups.
