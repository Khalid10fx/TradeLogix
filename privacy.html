Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

1. What this covers

TradeCycle is a trading journal and educational tool. This policy describes how information is handled by the current web/standalone version of the product.

2. Information you provide

TradeCycle may store your name, optional email address, trades, notes, screenshots, market information, calculations, preferences, backups, password-lock information and Premium/ad-removal state.

3. Local storage

Core journal information is designed to remain in browser or device storage. The optional password lock stores a SHA-256 hash rather than the password itself. This is a convenience lock, not encryption or a secure account system.

4. CSV imports

Selected broker CSV files are processed in the browser for import into your journal. They are intended to remain local unless you separately use a feature that sends information to a network service.

5. Third-party services

Depending on the enabled features and version, TradeCycle may interact with services such as market-data providers, Web3Forms, Buy Me a Coffee, advertising providers, hosting providers, or future broker integrations. Those services may process technical or submitted information under their own privacy policies and terms.

6. Broker connections

Live broker auto-sync may be released progressively. Do not enter broker credentials into unsupported fields or unofficial TradeCycle versions.

7. Retention and deletion

Local information generally remains until you delete it or clear the relevant browser/app storage. Exported backups are separate copies and must be deleted separately. Information held by third-party providers is governed by their retention rules.

8. Security

No browser storage, downloaded application, or internet service can be guaranteed completely secure. Protect your device, backups and any account credentials used with third-party services.

9. Changes

This policy may be updated as TradeCycle develops. The date above identifies the current version.

10. Contact

Use the official TradeCycle support/contact channel published with the version you are using for privacy questions.
