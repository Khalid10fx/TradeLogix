Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

1. What this policy covers. This Privacy Policy explains how the current TradeCycle trading-journal application handles information when you use the standalone HTML/web version, including when you open it in a browser or install it as a home-screen web app. TradeCycle is designed to keep your journal usable on your device. This policy describes the current product behavior and may be updated when the product changes.

2. Information you choose to enter. TradeCycle may store information you provide, including your name, optional email address, trade entries, dates, markets/pairs, direction, session, bias, setups, entry/stop/target/exit values, P&L and R results, outcomes, discipline grades, notes, lessons, screenshots and other journal content. You should not enter passwords, payment-card numbers, private keys, seed phrases, authentication codes or other highly sensitive credentials into journal fields.

3. Information stored on your device. The current app uses browser/device storage, including localStorage, for journal data, preferences, your name and optional email, password-lock information, premium/ad-removal state, backups and other app settings. A password lock uses a SHA-256 hash rather than storing the password itself. The lock is a convenience/privacy feature, not encryption and not a substitute for device security.

4. Journal images and backups. Screenshots attached to trades can be stored with the app's local data. Backup/export features are intended to let you save your own journal data. Treat exported backup files as sensitive because they may contain your trades, notes and screenshots. Anyone who obtains a backup may be able to read its contents.

5. CSV imports. Broker and prop-firm CSV files are processed in the browser by the current CSV importer. The importer is designed to read the selected file locally and does not intentionally upload the CSV to TradeCycle's servers. Imported information becomes part of your local journal data.

6. Third-party network services. Some features can contact services outside TradeCycle. The current build includes optional market-data requests to Alpha Vantage when you configure an API key; an email/form submission through Web3Forms when you request a paid ad-removal/unlock code; and third-party advertising scripts when ads are enabled. These providers may receive technical information such as your IP address, browser/device information, request information or the information you submit to them, according to their own privacy policies. TradeCycle does not control those third parties' practices.

7. Payments. The current Premium purchase flow directs users to Buy Me a Coffee. Payment-card and payment-account information is handled by the payment provider rather than stored in the TradeCycle journal. TradeCycle may receive or process the email address you use when requesting a Premium/ad-removal code. Review the payment provider's own terms and privacy policy before paying.

8. Broker connections. The current interface contains a Broker Connect area and a future connector hook, but the visible broker-selection flow currently states that live auto-sync is coming soon and does not collect broker passwords. Do not provide broker credentials unless a future TradeCycle version clearly identifies a secure supported connection and explains what information is exchanged.

9. How TradeCycle uses information. Information stored locally is used to provide the journal, calculations, statistics, charts, reminders, achievements, backups, personalization and other app features. Information submitted through an external service is used for the purpose of that feature, such as processing a Premium/ad-removal request or retrieving market data.

10. Sharing. The standalone app does not require you to create a central TradeCycle account for ordinary journaling. Your local journal is not automatically uploaded merely because you create a trade. However, information can leave your device when you deliberately use a feature that contacts a third-party service, when advertising is enabled, or when a future network-connected feature is used. Do not assume that information entered into a third-party form or service remains only on your device.

11. Retention and deletion. Local data generally remains until you delete it, clear the relevant browser/app storage, reset the journal, remove the app's site data, or otherwise overwrite/delete it. Exported backups are separate copies and must be deleted separately. Data sent to third-party services is subject to those providers' retention practices.

12. Security. TradeCycle uses reasonable client-side measures available to a standalone web application, but no device, browser storage system or internet transmission can be guaranteed completely secure. Keep your operating system, browser and device protected, and do not share backup files or passwords.

13. Children's privacy. TradeCycle is a general-purpose trading journal and is not designed as a service for children. Do not use the service to collect or store children's personal information.

14. Changes. This policy may be revised when TradeCycle gains new features, third-party integrations, payment systems or account functionality. The “Last updated” date will identify the current version shown in the app.

15. Contact. For privacy questions or requests, use the official TradeCycle support/contact channel provided with the version of TradeCycle you are using or on its official website/app listing. Do not send payment-card numbers, passwords or private keys in a support request.
